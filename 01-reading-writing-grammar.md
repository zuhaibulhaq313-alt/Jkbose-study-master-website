# English Foundation — Reading, Writing and Grammar
## Reading comprehension method
1. Read the questions first to know what to look for.
2. Read the passage carefully and identify the central idea.
3. Underline evidence, names, dates and linking words.
4. Answer in your own words unless exact wording is requested.
5. Check that each answer addresses the question and does not add unsupported information.

## Parts of speech
- Noun: names a person, place, thing or idea.
- Pronoun: replaces a noun.
- Verb: expresses action or state.
- Adjective: describes a noun/pronoun.
- Adverb: modifies a verb, adjective or adverb.
- Preposition: shows relationships, e.g. in, on, under, between.
- Conjunction: connects words or clauses.
- Interjection: expresses a sudden feeling.

## Tenses: basic pattern
- Simple present: subject + base verb (add -s/-es for he/she/it).
- Present continuous: am/is/are + verb-ing.
- Simple past: past form of verb.
- Past continuous: was/were + verb-ing.
- Simple future: will + base verb.
Choose tense based on time and meaning, not by memorising names alone.

## Subject–verb agreement
Singular subject generally takes singular verb: “The student writes.” Plural subject generally takes plural verb: “The students write.” In “Each of the players is ready,” the subject is “each.”
Common error: “She do her work” → “She does her work.”

## Paragraph writing
Use a topic sentence, 3–5 supporting points, examples, and a concluding sentence. Keep one main idea per paragraph. Use linking words such as first, however, therefore and finally.

## Letter/email basics
Include an appropriate greeting, clear purpose, polite tone, organised details and suitable closing. Follow the exact format taught by your school and prescribed textbook.

## Practice
1. Identify the verb: “The birds flew across the valley.”
2. Correct: “He go to school every day.”
3. Change to past: “They play football.”
4. Write a 100-word paragraph on the value of education.
5. Read a short passage and write its main idea in one sentence.
## Answers
1. flew.
2. He goes to school every day.
3. They played football.
