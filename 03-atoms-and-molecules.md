# Science — Atoms and Molecules
## Laws of chemical combination
- Law of conservation of mass: in a chemical reaction, total mass of reactants equals total mass of products in a closed system.
- Law of constant proportions: a pure compound always contains the same elements in the same fixed proportion by mass.

## Atoms, molecules and ions
An atom is the smallest unit of an element that takes part in chemical reactions. A molecule is a group of atoms chemically bonded and capable of independent existence. An ion is a charged particle formed by loss or gain of electrons.
- Cation: positive ion, e.g. Na⁺.
- Anion: negative ion, e.g. Cl⁻.
Atoms are conserved in ordinary chemical reactions; bonds rearrange.

## Symbols, formulae and valency
Chemical symbols represent elements. A chemical formula gives the types and relative numbers of atoms.
Common valencies/charges (check prescribed table): H⁺ 1, Na⁺ 1, Mg²⁺ 2, Al³⁺ 3, Cl⁻ 1, O²⁻ 2, N³⁻ 3, OH⁻ 1, NO₃⁻ 1, SO₄²⁻ 2, CO₃²⁻ 2, NH₄⁺ 1.
Examples: magnesium chloride MgCl₂; aluminium oxide Al₂O₃; calcium hydroxide Ca(OH)₂. Use brackets when a polyatomic ion occurs more than once.

## Molecular mass and mole idea
Molecular mass is the sum of the atomic masses in a molecule. Formula unit mass is used for ionic compounds.
Example: H₂O = 2(1)+16 = 18 u; CO₂ = 12+2(16)=44 u.
One mole contains approximately 6.022×10²³ entities; use this when the topic is included in your syllabus.

## Practice
1. State the conservation of mass law.
2. Write formulae for sodium oxide, calcium chloride and aluminium chloride.
3. Calculate molecular mass of NH₃ using N=14, H=1.
4. Explain the difference between cation and anion.
5. Find the formula unit mass of CaCO₃ using Ca=40, C=12, O=16.
## Answers
1. Total mass remains conserved in a chemical reaction in a closed system.
2. Na₂O, CaCl₂, AlCl₃.
3. 14+3=17 u.
4. Cation is positive; anion is negative.
5. 40+12+48=100 u.
