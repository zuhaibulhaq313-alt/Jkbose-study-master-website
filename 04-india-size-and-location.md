# Geography — India: Size and Location
## Location and extent
India lies in the Northern and Eastern Hemispheres. The mainland extends roughly from 8°4′N to 37°6′N latitude and 68°7′E to 97°25′E longitude (islands extend farther south). The Tropic of Cancer crosses the country near its middle.
India's central location at the head of the Indian Ocean has historically supported links with West Asia, Africa, Europe and Southeast Asia.

## Size and time
India is a large country with varied relief and climate. The standard meridian for Indian Standard Time is 82°30′E longitude. The time difference across longitudes makes one standard time useful for administration.
India's north-south and east-west extent create regional differences in climate, daylight and local time.

## Neighbours
India shares land borders with several countries, including Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar; Afghanistan is listed in some official geographical descriptions of India's claimed northwestern boundary.
