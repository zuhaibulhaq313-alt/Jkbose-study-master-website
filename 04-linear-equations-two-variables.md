# Mathematics — Linear Equations in Two Variables
## Standard form
A linear equation in two variables can be written ax + by + c = 0, where a and b are not both zero. A solution is an ordered pair (x,y) satisfying the equation.
One linear equation generally has infinitely many solutions.

## Finding solutions
For 2x+y=5, rearrange y=5−2x. Choosing x=0 gives y=5; x=1 gives y=3; x=2 gives y=1. Plot these points and join them to get a straight line.
Every point on the line satisfies the equation, and every solution lies on the line.

## Real-life model
If apples cost ₹20 each and pencils cost ₹5 each, the total cost for x apples and y pencils is 20x+5y. A budget of ₹100 gives 20x+5y=100. Interpret solutions using non-negative whole numbers if items cannot be fractional.

## Practice
1. Find three solutions of x+y=6.
2. Does (2,1) satisfy 3x−y=5?
3. Write y in terms of x for 4x+2y=8.
4. Make a value table and sketch x−y=2.
5. Explain why a single linear equation in two variables has many solutions.
## Answers
1. (0,6), (2,4), (6,0), among others.
2. Yes: 3(2)−1=5.
3. y=4−2x.
5. Many values of x can be paired with a corresponding y that satisfies the equation.
