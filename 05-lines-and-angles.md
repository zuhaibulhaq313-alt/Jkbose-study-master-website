# Mathematics — Lines and Angles
## Key terms
- Line: extends indefinitely in both directions.
- Line segment: part of a line with two endpoints.
- Ray: has one endpoint and extends indefinitely in one direction.
- Angle: formed by two rays with a common endpoint.
- Complementary angles sum to 90°; supplementary angles sum to 180°.
- Vertically opposite angles are equal.
- A linear pair sums to 180°.
- If a transversal cuts parallel lines, corresponding and alternate interior angles are equal; co-interior angles on the same side sum to 180°.

## Worked examples
1. If one angle in a linear pair is 68°, the other is 180°−68°=112°.
2. If vertically opposite angles are (3x+10)° and 100°, then 3x+10=100, so x=30.
3. If two complementary angles are x and 2x, then 3x=90, so x=30° and 2x=60°.

## Practice
1. Find the supplement of 47°.
2. Find the complement of 28°.
3. Vertically opposite angles are (2x+15)° and 75°. Find x.
4. Two co-interior angles are (x+20)° and 2x°. Find x.
5. State the angle relationship used when alternate interior angles are equal.
## Answers
1. 133°.
2. 62°.
3. 2x+15=75 → x=30.
4. x+20+2x=180 → x=160/3 (53⅓°); the other angle is 106⅔°.
5. The lines are parallel when the converse conditions apply; check the exact theorem taught in the prescribed text.
