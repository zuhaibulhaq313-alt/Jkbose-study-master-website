# Geography — Physical Features of India
## Major physiographic divisions
1. Himalayan Mountains: young fold mountains in the north; influence climate and river systems.
2. Northern Plains: formed largely by alluvial deposits of the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra systems; fertile and densely populated.
3. Peninsular Plateau: ancient stable landmass with broad plateaus, hills and mineral resources.
4. Indian Desert: arid region in the west with low rainfall and sparse vegetation.
5. Coastal Plains: western and eastern coastal strips with differences in width and river-delta development.
6. Islands: Andaman and Nicobar in the Bay of Bengal; Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea.

## Why relief matters
Relief influences rainfall, temperature, soil, agriculture, transport, settlement and biodiversity. Mountains can act as climatic barriers; plains support intensive agriculture; plateaus contain many mineral resources; coasts support ports and fishing.
