# Mathematics — Triangles
## Core ideas
A triangle has three sides and three angles. Interior angles sum to 180°. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
## Congruence criteria
SSS: three corresponding sides equal; SAS: two sides and included angle equal; ASA: two angles and included side equal; RHS: right angle, hypotenuse and one side equal for right triangles. Corresponding parts of congruent triangles are equal (CPCT).
## Inequalities
The sum of any two sides is greater than the third side. The greater angle lies opposite the greater side, and conversely.
## Practice
1. Two angles are 50° and 60°. Find the third.
2. Can sides 2, 3, 6 form a triangle? Why?
3. State SSS and SAS criteria.
4. An exterior angle has opposite interior angles 35° and 70°. Find it.
## Answers
1. 70°. 2. No, 2+3 is not greater than 6. 4. 105°.
