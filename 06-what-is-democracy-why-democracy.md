# Political Science — What Is Democracy? Why Democracy?
## Definition and essential features
Democracy is a form of government in which rulers are chosen by the people through elections. In a representative democracy, people elect representatives to make decisions on their behalf.
Important features include free and fair elections, universal adult franchise, political competition, rule of law, protection of rights, and accountability of government.

## Why democracy?
Potential strengths: elected leaders can be held accountable; citizens have a peaceful way to change government; public discussion can improve decisions; equality of political status is recognised; rights and dignity may be better protected.
Challenges: decision-making can be slow; money or misinformation can distort elections; majority rule can threaten minorities if rights are not protected. Democracy requires institutions, participation and safeguards—not elections alone.
