# Political Science — Constitutional Design
## Constitution
A constitution is the fundamental set of rules and principles by which a country is governed. It defines institutions, allocates powers, limits government and protects rights. A constitution can also express shared goals and values.
South Africa's transition from apartheid is often studied to understand negotiated change, equality and constitutional rights. India's Constitution was drafted by the Constituent Assembly and came into effect on 26 January 1950.

## Preamble values
The Indian Preamble describes India as Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic and expresses Justice, Liberty, Equality and Fraternity. Learn the meaning of each term using your prescribed textbook and teacher's explanation.
- Sovereign: independent in internal and external decision-making.
- Democratic: government based on people's political participation.
- Republic: head of state is not a hereditary monarch.
- Justice: social, economic and political fairness.
- Liberty: freedoms protected within constitutional limits.
- Equality: equal status and opportunity.
- Fraternity: shared belonging and dignity.
