# Mathematics — Quadrilaterals
A quadrilateral's interior angles sum to 360°. A parallelogram has opposite sides parallel and equal; opposite angles equal; diagonals bisect each other. A rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles. A rhombus has four equal sides. A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus. A trapezium/trapezoid has a pair of parallel sides according to the textbook convention.
## Practice
1. Three angles of a quadrilateral are 80°, 90° and 100°. Find the fourth.
2. State two properties of a parallelogram.
3. How are square, rectangle and rhombus related?
## Answers
1. 90°. 2. Opposite sides parallel/equal; diagonals bisect each other. 3. A square is a special rectangle and rhombus; rectangle and rhombus each have additional defining properties.
