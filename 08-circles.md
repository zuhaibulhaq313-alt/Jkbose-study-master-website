# Mathematics — Circles
A circle is the set of points at a fixed distance (radius) from a centre. Diameter = 2r. A chord joins two points on a circle; diameter is the longest chord. A perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord. Equal chords subtend equal angles at the centre (within the standard theorem context). A tangent touches the circle at one point and is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
## Practice
1. Find diameter if radius is 7 cm.
2. What is the relationship between tangent and radius at contact?
3. Define chord and arc.
## Answers
1. 14 cm. 2. They are perpendicular. 3. Chord is a segment joining two points on the circle; arc is part of the circumference.
