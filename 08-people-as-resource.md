# Economics — People as Resource
## Human capital
People become a resource when education, training and health improve their knowledge, skills and productivity. Spending on education and health can benefit individuals and society over time.
Economic activities include market activities (for pay/profit) and non-market activities (often for self-consumption or household use). Both can contribute to wellbeing, although unpaid work may be undercounted in conventional measures.

## Unemployment
Unemployment occurs when people who are willing and able to work at prevailing conditions cannot find work. Seasonal unemployment occurs during parts of the year; disguised unemployment occurs when more people are engaged in work than needed, so some workers' removal would not reduce output.
Education and health improve capability but must be supported by appropriate opportunities, fair access and job creation.
