# Mathematics — Heron's Formula
For a triangle with side lengths a,b,c, semi-perimeter s=(a+b+c)/2. Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]. Use consistent units and check that the three lengths can form a triangle.
## Example
Sides 3,4,5: s=6. Area=√[6×3×2×1]=√36=6 square units.
## Practice
1. Find semi-perimeter for sides 5,6,7.
2. Find area of a 3-4-5 triangle.
## Answers
1. 9. 2. 6 square units.
