# Economics — Poverty as a Challenge
## Understanding poverty
Poverty is not only low income; it can involve inadequate food, housing, education, healthcare, sanitation, security and opportunities. Poverty measures depend on a defined threshold and the data used, so use the figures from the prescribed textbook for examination questions.

## Causes and responses
Possible causes include low and insecure income, unemployment, unequal access to assets and education, social exclusion, poor health, and vulnerability to shocks. Responses can include employment opportunities, quality education, healthcare, social protection, rural development, fair access to resources and effective public services.
Avoid blaming individuals for structural problems; poverty is shaped by both personal circumstances and social/economic conditions.
