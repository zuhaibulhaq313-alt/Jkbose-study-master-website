# Geography — Climate
Climate is the long-term pattern of weather in a region; weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions. India's climate is influenced by latitude, altitude, pressure and winds, distance from the sea, relief and monsoon circulation. Seasonal patterns commonly studied include cold weather, hot weather, advancing monsoon and retreating monsoon. Rainfall varies across regions because of wind direction, relief and distance from moisture sources. Use the exact map, dates and terminology in your prescribed textbook.
