# Mathematics — Statistics and Probability Foundation
Statistics organises and interprets data. A frequency table records how often values occur. Mean = sum of observations / number of observations. Median is the middle value after sorting (average of two middle values for an even count). Mode is the most frequent value. Probability in an equally likely finite sample space = favourable outcomes / total outcomes; its value is from 0 to 1.
## Example
For 2,3,3,7, mean=15/4=3.75, median=(3+3)/2=3, mode=3.
## Practice
1. Find mean of 4,6,8.
2. Find median of 1,3,8,10.
3. A fair die is rolled. What is probability of an even number?
## Answers
1. 6. 2. 5.5. 3. 3/6=1/2.
