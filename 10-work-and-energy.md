# Science — Work and Energy
## Work
In the simple case of a constant force parallel to displacement, work W=Fs. If force is perpendicular to displacement, work is zero. SI unit: joule (J).
Energy is capacity to do work. Kinetic energy KE=½mv². Gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface PE=mgh.
Power = work/time; SI unit watt (W).

## Conservation of energy
Energy is not created or destroyed in an isolated system; it changes form. In a falling object, gravitational potential energy decreases while kinetic energy increases, ignoring air resistance.
Commercial unit of electrical energy is kilowatt-hour (kWh); 1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J.

## Worked example
A 2 kg object moves at 3 m/s. KE=½×2×9=9 J.
A 10 N force moves an object 4 m in the force direction. Work=40 J.

## Practice
1. Calculate KE for m=4 kg, v=5 m/s.
2. A 20 N force moves an object 3 m. Find work.
3. A 100 J task takes 5 s. Find power.
4. Explain conservation of energy using a swinging pendulum.
5. Convert 2 kWh to joules.
## Answers
1. 50 J.
2. 60 J.
3. 20 W.
5. 7.2×10⁶ J.
