# Geography — Drainage
A drainage basin is the area drained by a river and its tributaries; a water divide separates neighbouring basins. Himalayan rivers are generally perennial because they receive water from rainfall and snowmelt; many Peninsular rivers depend more strongly on seasonal rainfall. River systems shape valleys, floodplains, deltas and estuaries. Rivers support water supply, irrigation, transport and ecosystems but may also cause floods and pollution.
