# Science — Improvement in Food Resources
Food security depends on availability, access and proper use of food. Crop variety improvement aims to improve yield, quality, resistance to pests/diseases and tolerance to environmental stresses. Crop production management includes nutrient management, irrigation and cropping patterns. Organic manure adds organic matter; fertilisers supply concentrated nutrients. Both require responsible use. Crop protection includes prevention and control of weeds, pests and diseases. Storage losses may be caused by moisture, insects, rodents and microorganisms. Animal husbandry involves scientific care, breeding, feeding and disease prevention for livestock and poultry; fisheries and beekeeping can also support livelihoods.
