# Political Science — Working of Institutions
Democratic institutions divide and organise public power. The legislature makes laws, the executive implements them and the judiciary interprets law and resolves disputes. In parliamentary systems, the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the elected lower house, while the head of state has a constitutional role. Institutions create procedures and checks so that decisions are not based only on one person's wishes. The exact structure and terms should be studied from the prescribed textbook.
