# Political Science — Democratic Rights
Rights are justified claims protected by law or accepted moral principles. Rights protect dignity and freedom and limit misuse of power. Constitutional rights can be enforced through courts, according to the country's legal framework. Rights are not unlimited: lawful restrictions may apply to protect others and public interests, but restrictions should follow constitutional safeguards. Learn the specific rights, articles and examples prescribed for your class and session.
