# Economics — Food Security
Food security means people have reliable physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food. Its common dimensions are availability, accessibility and affordability. Vulnerability can increase during crop failure, disasters, unemployment or price rises. Public distribution systems, buffer stocks and nutrition programmes are policy tools used in India; their operation and current details should be checked against the prescribed text and official sources. Food security also depends on storage, transport, livelihoods and equitable access.
