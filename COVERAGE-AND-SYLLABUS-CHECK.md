# Coverage and syllabus check

This foundation pack includes introductory material for Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, Urdu support and Computer Foundations. Some commonly taught Class 9 chapters are included, but this is not yet a verified, exhaustive match to a particular JKBOSE session's prescribed textbook contents.

Before treating any guide as exam-complete:
1. Open the current JKBOSE syllabus for your session.
2. Compare each official chapter title with this library.
3. Add missing chapters from your prescribed books and teacher's list.
4. Use the textbook's exact definitions, diagrams, theorems and exercise questions where required.
5. Add official previous-year questions only from verified papers.

This pack contains original explanatory notes and practice questions, not the complete copyrighted textbook.
