# Class 11 | Arts / Humanities | History

> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## Historical evidence

Historians use archaeological remains, inscriptions, texts, oral histories, and material culture. Sources must be interpreted in context and cross-checked.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Chronology and change

Make timelines and compare political structures, social groups, economies, and cultural practices across periods.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Cause and consequence

Separate long-term conditions, immediate triggers, key actors, and outcomes. Avoid explaining complex events through a single cause.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Writing history answers

Start with a direct response, organise evidence into points, explain significance, and end with a short conclusion.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. Why compare multiple historical sources?**

Answer: To test reliability, recognise different viewpoints, and build a better-supported interpretation.

## Exam practice

1. Define two key terms from this unit.
2. Explain one major idea using an example or labelled diagram where relevant.
3. Attempt one short-answer and one long-answer question from your current textbook.
4. Check your answer against the textbook, improve it, and add mistakes to an error log.
