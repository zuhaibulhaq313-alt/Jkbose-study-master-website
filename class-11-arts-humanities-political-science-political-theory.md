Studies and evaluates political ideas, values, institutions and arguments. Learn to define a concept, compare positions, and support conclusions.

## Equality and social justice

Equality concerns equal moral worth and fair treatment; social justice examines fair distribution, opportunities, and protection from disadvantage.

## Rights and citizenship

Rights are justified claims recognised by ethical principles or law. Citizenship combines membership with rights, duties, and participation.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. What is political theory?**

Answer: Systematic study and evaluation of political ideas, values, and institutions.

**Q2. What does social justice ask us to consider?**

Answer: Whether benefits, opportunities, burdens, and protections are distributed fairly.
