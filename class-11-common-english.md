# Class 11 | Common | English

> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## Comprehension

Identify the main idea, tone, inference, and evidence. Keep answers precise and relevant.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Writing skills

Practise prescribed notices, formal letters, reports, speeches, and paragraphs. Check audience, format, and word limit.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Literature

For each chapter and poem, prepare summary, theme, character or speaker notes, vocabulary, devices, and textbook answers.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. What is tone in a text?**

Answer: The writer's or speaker's attitude toward the subject.

**Q2. How should a literature answer be supported?**

Answer: With relevant details or evidence from the prescribed text.

## Exam practice

1. Define two key terms from this unit.
2. Explain one major idea using an example or labelled diagram where relevant.
3. Attempt one short-answer and one long-answer question from your current textbook.
4. Check your answer against the textbook, improve it, and add mistakes to an error log.
