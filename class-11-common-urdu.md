> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## Prose

Read the prescribed lesson and prepare a concise summary, central idea, vocabulary, and short/long answers.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Poetry

Study the poet, theme, imagery, key terms, and explanation of prescribed verses using the textbook and teacher's notes.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Grammar and writing

Practise the grammar and writing formats listed in the current syllabus. Keep a vocabulary notebook and revise spellings.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. What should a lesson summary contain?**

Answer: The main ideas or events, stated briefly and accurately in your own words.

## Exam practice

1. Define two key terms from this unit.
2. Explain one major idea using an example or labelled diagram where relevant.
3. Attempt one short-answer and one long-answer question from your current textbook.
4. Check your answer against the textbook, improve it, and add mistakes to an error log.
