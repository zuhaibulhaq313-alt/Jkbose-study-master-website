# Class 9 | Common | English

> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## Reading comprehension

Read the questions first, then the passage. Identify the central idea, supporting evidence, tone, and meaning of words from context. Answer in your own words unless a quotation is requested.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Grammar

Revise parts of speech, tense consistency, subject–verb agreement, articles, prepositions, punctuation, and direct/indirect speech as prescribed. Keep a notebook of errors and corrected examples.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Writing skills

Use a clear structure: opening/purpose, organised body, and closing. For letters, follow the format taught at school. For paragraphs, use one main idea per paragraph and proofread spelling.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Literature

For every prose lesson or poem, prepare a 100-word summary, theme, important characters or speaker, vocabulary, literary devices, and answers to textbook questions.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. What is the central idea of a passage?**

Answer: The main point or message developed by the passage.

**Q2. What makes a paragraph coherent?**

Answer: A clear main idea, logically ordered supporting sentences, and transitions.

## Exam practice

1. Define two key terms from this unit.
2. Explain one major idea using an example or labelled diagram where relevant.
3. Attempt one short-answer and one long-answer question from your current textbook.
4. Check your answer against the textbook, improve it, and add mistakes to an error log.
