# Class 9 | Common | Mathematics

> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## Number systems

Distinguish natural, whole, integer, rational, and irrational numbers. A rational number can be written p/q where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0. Practise representing real numbers and laws of exponents.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Polynomials

Know terms, coefficients, degree, zeroes, and identities. Practise factorisation and verify identities by expanding both sides.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Linear equations and geometry

Show every algebraic step. In geometry, write the given facts, the statement to prove, a labelled diagram, and a reason for each step.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Mensuration and statistics

Write the formula before substituting values. Include units. For data questions, organise observations and check whether the answer is reasonable.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. Can a rational number have a terminating decimal?**

Answer: Yes. Its reduced denominator has only 2 and/or 5 as prime factors.

**Q2. What should a geometry proof include?**

Answer: Given facts, a labelled diagram, logical steps, and reasons.
