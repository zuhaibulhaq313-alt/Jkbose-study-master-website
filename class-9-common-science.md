## Matter

Matter has mass and occupies space. Compare solids, liquids and gases by particle spacing, movement, shape, and volume. Explain diffusion and changes of state using particle motion.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Atoms and molecules

Use symbols and formulae correctly. Practise conservation of mass, valency, and balancing simple chemical equations.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Cell biology

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Learn membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, and differences between plant and animal cells.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Motion and force

Speed = distance/time; velocity includes direction; acceleration is change in velocity/time. Use units and label axes on graphs.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Environment

Explain food chains, producers, consumers, decomposers, resource conservation, and how human activity affects ecosystems.
