# Class 9 | Common | Social Science

> Original revision guide. Match every topic with your current prescribed textbook and syllabus.

## History

Create timelines. For each event, note background, causes, key developments, groups involved, and consequences. Avoid memorising dates without understanding significance.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Geography

Practise maps and use location words accurately. Explain physical processes step by step, linking causes to visible outcomes.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Political science

Learn terms such as democracy, constitution, rights, equality, and institutions. Support explanations with examples.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Economics

Distinguish resources, production, consumption, markets, and livelihoods. Use simple real-life examples to explain concepts.

**Revision task:** Write a 3–5 point summary, then answer a textbook question without looking at your notes.

## Quick self-check

**Q1. What is a constitution?**

Answer: The fundamental rules and principles by which a country is governed.

**Q2. Why use a timeline in history?**

Answer: To organise events chronologically and understand sequence and change.
