# How to use this library effectively

## Daily study cycle
1. **Preview (5–10 minutes):** Read the chapter headings and learning objectives.
2. **Learn (25–40 minutes):** Study a small section from the prescribed textbook.
3. **Recall (10 minutes):** Close the book and write the main ideas from memory.
4. **Practise (15–30 minutes):** Solve textbook questions or numerical problems.
5. **Correct (5–10 minutes):** Mark errors and write why the correct answer works.

## Weekly review
- Revisit notes from the previous six days.
- Take a short closed-book test.
- Redo incorrect questions without looking at solutions.
- Plan the next week around the weakest topics.

## Subject-specific methods
- **Mathematics/Physics/Chemistry/Accountancy:** practise problems, show steps, check units and maintain an error log.
- **Biology/Geography/History/Political Science:** use labelled diagrams, maps, timelines, comparisons and cause–effect charts.
- **English/Urdu:** practise reading, writing formats, vocabulary and evidence-based literature answers.
- **Economics/Business Studies:** define terms precisely, use examples, and practise diagrams/case-based answers where relevant.

## Exam readiness checklist
- Confirm the current official syllabus and textbook chapters.
- Practise the prescribed question pattern and time limits.
- Prepare diagrams, maps, formulas, definitions, and key terms.
- Sleep adequately and avoid last-minute all-night study.
