# Mixed Practice Set 1 — Class 9 Foundation
Try the questions without notes. Give yourself 1 mark per correct short answer and 2 marks for explanations/calculations.

## Mathematics
1. Simplify √72.
2. Find the degree of 5x³−2x+1.
3. Find the complement of 37°.
4. Find the mean of 5, 7, 9, 11.
5. Find three solutions to x+y=4.

## Science
6. Define diffusion.
7. Give the formula for acceleration.
8. Calculate force for m=6 kg and a=2 m/s².
9. Name the cell organelle where protein synthesis occurs.
10. State the conservation of mass law.

## Social Science
11. Give two causes of the French Revolution.
12. Define democracy.
13. Name India's Standard Meridian.
14. What is human capital?
15. Explain one role of a constitution.

## English and digital skills

18. Give two examples of input devices.

20. Write a three-sentence summary of a lesson you studied this week.
