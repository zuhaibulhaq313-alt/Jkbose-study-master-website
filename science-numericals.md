# Science Numericals — Formula Practice
## Formula sheet
- Speed = distance / time
- Acceleration = (final velocity − initial velocity) / time
- Force = mass × acceleration
- Momentum = mass × velocity
- Weight = mass × gravitational acceleration
- Work (parallel force) = force × displacement
- Kinetic energy = ½ × mass × speed²
- Potential energy near Earth's surface = mass × g × height
- Power = work / time
- Wave speed = frequency × wavelength
- Density = mass / volume (if included in the current course)

## Questions
1. A cyclist covers 240 m in 40 s. Find average speed.
2. A vehicle changes velocity from 4 m/s to 16 m/s in 6 s. Find acceleration.
3. A 7 kg object accelerates at 2 m/s². Find net force.
4. Find momentum of a 3 kg object moving at 8 m/s.
5. Find weight of 12 kg mass for g=10 m/s².
6. A 15 N force moves a box 5 m in the force direction. Find work.
7. Find kinetic energy for m=2 kg and v=6 m/s.
8. A 200 J task is completed in 10 s. Find power.
9. A wave has frequency 200 Hz and wavelength 1.5 m. Find speed.
10. A sample has mass 100 g and volume 20 cm³. Find density.

## Answers
1. 6 m/s.
2. 2 m/s².
3. 14 N.
4. 24 kg m/s.
5. 120 N.
6. 75 J.
7. 36 J.
8. 20 W.
9. 300 m/s.
10. 5 g/cm³.
