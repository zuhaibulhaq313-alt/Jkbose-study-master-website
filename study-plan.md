# Class 9 Foundation Study Plan

## Daily study block (adapt to your school timetable)
- 20 min: recall yesterday's lesson without looking at notes.
- 40 min: learn one new concept.
- 30 min: solve questions or write answers.
- 10 min: record mistakes and plan the next session.

## Weekly rotation
- Monday: Mathematics + English
- Tuesday: Science + Social Science
- Wednesday: Mathematics + language
- Thursday: Science + Social Science
- Friday: Mathematics + English writing/grammar
- Saturday: revise the week's chapters and complete practice questions
- Sunday: short self-test, correct errors, and rest.

## Chapter completion checklist
- [ ] I can explain the topic in my own words.
- [ ] I know the definitions and key terms.
- [ ] I can solve the worked examples without looking.
- [ ] I attempted every practice question.
- [ ] I corrected my mistakes and can explain why my first answer was wrong.
- [ ] I revised the chapter after 1 day and again after 1 week.

## How to prepare a written answer
1. Read the command word: define, explain, compare, give reasons, calculate.
2. Start with the direct answer.
3. Add a definition, formula, labelled diagram, example, or evidence when relevant.
4. Use short paragraphs or numbered points for clarity.
5. Check units, spelling, labels, and whether every part of the question was answered.
