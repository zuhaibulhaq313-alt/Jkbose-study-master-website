# Reusable Weekly Study Planner

| Day | Main topic | Practice task | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | New chapter / concept | 10–20 questions or worked examples | 10-minute recall |
| Tuesday | Continue chapter | Textbook questions | Correct mistakes |
| Wednesday | Second subject | Diagram/map/formula practice | Flashcards |
| Thursday | Continue second subject | Short and long answers | Closed-book summary |
| Friday | Weakest topic | Targeted practice | Update error log |
| Saturday | Mixed revision | Timed mini-test | Check answers |
| Sunday | Weekly recap | Redo incorrect questions | Plan next week |

Adapt the amount of work to your school timetable, health, and upcoming exams. Regular, focused study is more useful than an unrealistic timetable.
