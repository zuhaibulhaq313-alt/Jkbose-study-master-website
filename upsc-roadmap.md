# UPSC Foundation Roadmap

This is a long-term foundation guide, not a promise of selection or a substitute for the official notification.

## Stage 1 — School foundations
- Read school textbooks carefully and learn to explain concepts in your own words.
- Build reading comprehension, writing clarity, map skills, timelines, and basic numeracy.
- Keep a simple current-affairs notebook with one page per issue.

## Stage 2 — Build core subjects
- **Polity:** Constitution, rights, Parliament, executive, judiciary, federalism, local government.
- **History:** chronology, major movements, causes, consequences, and sources.
- **Geography:** physical processes, India maps, population, resources, and human geography.
- **Economy:** growth, inflation, budget, banking, employment, poverty, and development indicators.
- **Environment:** ecosystems, biodiversity, climate, conservation, and sustainability.
- **Science and technology:** school-level foundations and reliable explanations of major applications.

## Stage 3 — Skills
- Practise reading comprehension and logical reasoning.
- Write structured answers: introduction, organised points, examples/evidence, conclusion.
- Review mistakes in a notebook and retest after a few days.

## Weekly routine template
- 4 days: core subject learning and recall.
- 1 day: current affairs and issue notes.
- 1 day: practice questions and answer writing.
- 1 day: revision, rest, and planning.

## Important
Check the latest UPSC notification and official syllabus for eligibility, exam structure, dates, and rules. Do not rely on old dates or social-media claims.
